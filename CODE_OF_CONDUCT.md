# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome here. In issues, pull requests and anywhere else we meet:

- Be respectful, and assume good intent.
- Keep to the topic, and let others finish their point.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, and nothing that targets a person for who they are.

Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads or block accounts that break these rules. Report a problem
to ops@geniffy.com; reports are kept confidential.
